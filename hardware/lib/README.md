# Footprint and pinout evidence

This file records where each custom footprint's geometry and numbering came
from. The footprints in `dongle.pretty/` are generated by
`hardware/design/gen_footprints.py`; do not edit them by hand.

## TC2030-MCP-NL (programming footprint, J3)

Source: Tag-Connect drawing "FOOTPRINT FOR TC2030-MCP-NL PLUG-OF-NAILS CABLE",
drawing number TC2030-MCP-NL-FP rev A, three sheets. Copy read on 2026-11-10:
<https://www.farnell.com/cad/3360979.pdf>

Sheet 1 shows the PCB top layer. The single alignment hole is on the left and
the pair of alignment holes on the right. Pins 1, 3 and 5 are the lower row;
pins 2, 4 and 6 are the upper row.

| Pad | x (mm) | y (mm, down) | Microchip ICSP signal | RJ12 pin | Used here as |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | −1.27 | +0.635 | MCLR / VPP | 6 | not connected |
| 2 | −1.27 | −0.635 | VDD | 5 | 3.3 V |
| 3 | 0 | +0.635 | GND | 4 | ground |
| 4 | 0 | −0.635 | PGD (ICSPDAT) | 3 | UPDI |
| 5 | +1.27 | +0.635 | PGC (ICSPCLK) | 2 | not connected |
| 6 | +1.27 | −0.635 | Unused / LVP | 1 | not connected |

Alignment holes: unplated, 0.991 mm, at (−2.54, 0), (+2.54, −1.016) and
(+2.54, +1.016). Contact pads: 0.787 mm round, no solder paste.

This is the same land pattern and the same pad numbering as KiCad's stock
footprint `Connector:Tag-Connect_TC2030-IDC-NL_2x03_P1.27mm_Vertical`, which
the generator copies. `test_tc2030_pad_positions` checks both.

Rules from the drawing's notes that the board layout must respect:

- No tracks or vias in the area between the two rows of contact pads.
- No other track or signal within 0.020 in (0.51 mm) of any contact pad.
- No solder paste on the contact pads.
- The TC2030-CLIP retaining board attaches to the alignment pins from
  underneath, so the underside behind the footprint stays clear.

The RJ12 column is from sheet 3 of the same drawing. It matters only if the
cable is ever buzzed out; the board follows the pad column.

## WirePads_1x02_Strain and WirePads_1x03_Strain (J1, J2)

Project-specific; there is no datasheet. Dimensions are the design
document's starting sizes (section 5.2):

| Feature | Size |
|---|---|
| Solder pad | plated hole, 1.0 mm drill, 1.8 mm round copper |
| Pad pitch | 2.54 mm |
| Strain-relief hole | unplated, 1.5 mm, 3.0 mm from its pad toward the board end |

A wire goes down through the strain-relief hole, along the underside, and up
through its pad to be soldered on the top. The 1.0 mm drill suits stranded
wire up to about 22 AWG; the 1.5 mm hole suits insulation up to about 1.4 mm.
These are estimates until the owner's actual cables are measured.
